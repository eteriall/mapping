# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and welcoming.
- Give and accept feedback with empathy.
- Focus discussion on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's personal information.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
trolling, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately using the contact method in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may
temporarily or permanently ban a contributor.

Maintainers will respect the reporter's privacy and will apply consequences
fairly and proportionately.
